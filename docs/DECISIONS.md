# Decision log

Owner decisions (D-xxx) and technical decisions (T-xxx). Each entry records what was decided, why, and
when. A decision is changed only by adding a new entry that supersedes it. Old entries are never
rewritten; they may carry a short note pointing to the entry that supersedes, refines or confirms them,
and typos and citations may be corrected in place. A superseded technical entry's status reads
"SUPERSEDED by X". A fully superseded entry is reduced to a one-line stub naming its replacement;
its full text stays in the git history (D-046).
