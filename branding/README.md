# EternalVR branding

The EternalVR name and the original design elements of the logo and banners (`eternalvr-logo*.png`,
`eternalvr-banner*.png`, `eternalvr-social-1280x640.png`, `launcher-header-1440x240.png`,
`eternalvr.ico`) are not covered by the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

The demon artwork in these images is fan art of id Software's character; no rights in it are claimed or
granted here. DOOM is a trademark of id Software / ZeniMax Media Inc. EternalVR is an unofficial fan
project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by id Software, Bethesda or ZeniMax.

You may use the unmodified logo to link to or write about EternalVR. Please do not use the name or the logo
for a fork, a different project or anything that could be taken for an official EternalVR release.

`eternalvr-social-1280x640.png` is the repository's social preview (GitHub: Settings, General, Social preview).

`eternalvr.ico` holds the pixel head at 16 to 64 px (16-32 scaled down from the detailed head, 48-64 hand-tuned) (the title bar, taskbar and Alt-Tab) and the
full logo at 128 and 256 px (Explorer's large views). `launcher-header-1440x240.png` is the launcher's
header strip, shown at 720x120.

`kofi-symbol-64.png` is Ko-fi's cup symbol (Ko-fi's brand asset `kofi_symbol.png`, only resized to 80x64), shown
beside the launcher's "Support EternalVR on Ko-fi" link. It belongs to Ko-fi Labs Limited, is not covered by
the MIT licence and is used only to link to the Ko-fi page.

`discord-symbol-blurple.png` is Discord's symbol in its colour, and `github-invertocat-black.png` is GitHub's mark (the
Invertocat) in black, each the brand's own file, unaltered: Discord's from its branding page (https://discord.com/branding,
"Symbol", the colour download, a zip holding `Discord_Symbol_Color/Discord-Symbol-Blurple.png`, SHA-256
`f6c1a66250d366d213789faefab59a9ec6ac6334e6c4be0af254680c148810dc`), GitHub's from its logos page
(https://github.com/logos, its download https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip, `PNG/GitHub_Invertocat_Black.png`,
SHA-256 `2a2f5cbcc74c7fa83c40127dd8b0e42c23f1157131aebe28492aa1ac27bbdc6d`); both fetched 2026-10-13, and downloaded
again from the same pages on 2026-10-16 with the same bytes. The launcher draws them, their proportions kept, at
the size of its text beside its "EternalVR on Discord" and "Report on GitHub" links, which open the EternalVR Discord
server's bug report channel and the GitHub issues of the EternalVR repository: the uses each brand's guidelines allow
(Discord: to say you have a Discord server; GitHub: a social button to link to your project). Discord and the Discord
logo are trademarks of Discord Inc.; GitHub and the Invertocat are trademarks of GitHub, Inc. Neither is covered by the
MIT licence, and neither company sponsors or endorses EternalVR.
